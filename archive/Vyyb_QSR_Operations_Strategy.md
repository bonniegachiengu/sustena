## 1. STRATEGIC FRAME: THE KENYAN JOLLIBEE THESIS

Jollibee beat McDonald's in the Philippines not by making better burgers — but by owning categories McDonald's structurally cannot enter. Sweet spaghetti. Garlic rice. Chickenjoy with signature gravy. These were invented category wins, not competitive copies.

**Vyyb's equivalent categories — own them, don't improve them:**

| Vyyb Category | Why KFC / McDonald's Cannot Follow |
|---|---|
| Ugali + stew combos | Off-menu for every global chain. No KFC has ever served ugali. |
| Pilau bowls | A cultural touchstone with flavour memory. McDonald's cannot do this credibly. |
| Smocha / Gizz Wrap | Street food formalized. No global chain has this format. |
| Uji Power | Kenyan breakfast drink with cultural legitimacy. Uncopiable by a foreign brand. |
| Kuku choma + fries | Grilled chicken with Kenyan spice profile and kachumbari. Not KFC's fried chicken. |
| Mandazi Dondo combo | Breakfast bundle with no global-chain equivalent in Kenya. |
| Mojito (Kenyan mint-lime-ginger) | Home-grown drink format — not a cocktail, not a soda. |

**The Jollibee operating principle applied to Vyyb:**
> Use global QSR operational systems (supply chain, franchise SOP, HACCP, KDS) and throw out their menu. Replace it with food built from Kenyan taste memory.

## 2. THE HIVE — COMMISSARY KITCHEN

### 2.1 Design Philosophy

The Hive does bulk work once. Outlets receive, heat, finish, and serve. An outlet chef never needs to know how to make pilau or knead chapati dough — the Hive has already done it. The outlet's job is 2–4 minute finish work and fast assembly.

**Minimum Hive footprint:** 150–300 sqm (Phase 1 can start at 100 sqm if tightly planned)

## 3. THE OUTLET — Fast-Finishing Service Machine

### 3.1 Design Philosophy

An outlet is NOT a kitchen. It is a heated assembly line. Every item arrives from the Hive either fully cooked or par-cooked. Outlet staff do 2–4 minute finish work only.

**Outlet size:** 15–40 sqm · **Staff (rush):** 5–6 · **Staff (quiet):** 3

Stations are colour-coded for KDS routing:
- 🟡 Yellow — Frying
- 🔴 Red — Grill
- 🟢 Green — Drinks
- 🔵 Blue — Dishes & Starch
- ⬜ Cream — Assembly
- 📦 Dispatch — Packaging
- 💳 POS — Cashier

**Daily (7 days a week):**
- Kachumbari — fresh batch every morning + top-up before dinner service
- Salad (raw cabbage + carrot mix) — morning prep only

- Blanched fries (chilled batch for outlets)
- Par-fried chicken (chilled batch for outlets)
- Kachumbari second batch
- Viazi karai batter-dip batch

- Master gravy (10L hub batch — per recipe: 3kg onions, 4kg tomatoes, etc.)
- Cooked legumes (beans, ndengu, minji) — rotate batches
- Cooked pilau, fried rice

**Weekly:**
- Sauce batches (5L each: BBQ, garlic, spicy, chilli)

- Par-fried chicken frozen batch (for buffer stock)

## 6. FRANCHISE READINESS FRAMEWORK

### 6.1 The Vyyb Franchise Promise (Jollibee Model Applied)

The franchise model only works if every outlet tastes **identical** to the Hive standard.
That means the Hive is not just a kitchen — it is the **taste guardian**.

**Three locks on consistency:**

1. **Hive controls flavour** — all sauces, marinades, dough, and bases are hub-made.
   A franchisee cannot change the spice blend. They can only finish and assemble.

2. **Recipes are portioned, not estimated** — every item has a gram-weight spec.
   Franchise training is on portion control, not recipe creation.

3. **HACCP is non-negotiable** — temperature logs, batch codes, and shelf-life tags
   are required for every dispatch. A franchise outlet that can't prove cold chain
   compliance loses its supply contract.
